## Could anything here change a headline number?
- **No critical issue was found.** No test, OOD or plant-B information enters training or threshold choice.
- **The numbers most at risk are the post-hoc B2 comparisons with the deployed hybrid (C-1).** The headline sentence "CNN / GRU / ANN hybrids are significantly more dependable than the deployed hybrid" compares models trained at twice the window density. The like-for-like statement should be against the **tuned LSTM hybrid** (same stride, same budget), where the B2 gains are +1.32 (ANN), +2.15 (CNN), +1.99 (GRU). From `b2_summary.txt`, these are significant pooled and still significant trace-clustered, so the architecture-not-classifier conclusion survives.
- **The p-values (C-2) are overstated.** Every pre-registered decision survives the clustered test, but five individual "*" marks do not (`results_audit.md` B1a).
