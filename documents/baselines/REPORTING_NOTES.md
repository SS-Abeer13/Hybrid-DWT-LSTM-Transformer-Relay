# What has to be stated next to the results of the reproduced methods (BENCHMARK_FAIRNESS_PLAN.md Part 2)

1. **High-frequency content of the plants.** The simulated transformers are lumped models without winding capacitances, and the network is solved at a fixed 25 us step with Backward Euler (powergui 40 kHz, data logged at 20 kHz). `hf_content.txt` (written by `tools/hf_content_check.py`) gives the fraction of the spectral energy of the differential current above 1.6, 5 and 8 kHz in the 20 ms after an event and in 20 ms of noise only: after events it is of the order of 1e-3 to 1e-5 (fundamental and inrush harmonics dominate), and the absolute energy above 5 kHz is of the order of the noise floor. Methods that rely on kHz-range fault transients (Simoes et al., Ngaopitakkul and Kunakorn, partly Abdusalam et al.) are therefore tested on data that carry little of the information they were designed to use; their results must be read with this limitation and must not be presented as a statement about those methods on real transformers. Adding winding capacitances is a larger plant change and is considered only if those methods look unfairly handicapped.
2. **Sampling rates.** Every method gets the currents at its own paper's rate (anti-aliased resampling from 20 kHz): 12.8 kHz (Simoes, 256 samples per cycle at 50 Hz instead of 15.36 kHz at 60 Hz), 5.6 kHz (Afrasiabi, 112 per cycle), 3.2 kHz (Key, 64 per cycle), 10 kHz (Abdusalam), 3 kHz (Alhamd), 20 kHz instead of 200 kHz (Ngaopitakkul and Kunakorn).
3. **Re-implementations.** All methods are our re-implementations, retrained on our data, using the paper's text; where the paper is silent a fill is listed in the method's spec. They are not the authors' code and their numbers can differ from the published ones.
4. **Model inconsistency to verify.** The `powergui` block of both v5 plants has Frequency = 60 Hz while the system runs at 50 Hz. The probable effect is nil (it governs initial-state and phasor tools), but it must be verified by running one trace with 50 Hz and one with 60 Hz and comparing the currents, and mentioned. Open: this check needs a MATLAB run and is queued after the high-rate re-simulation (one MATLAB at a time).
